# Recurring Sponsors & Medication

Satirical spots for products that make life more convenient in the worst way,
or medications that make you sicker. They repeat on purpose and the gags
accumulate (RADIO.md §7). Commercial announcer voice: `af_aoede`
(exaggerated read — the spots should sound like someone who believes *too* hard).

## 1. Glue-Free Glue
"Adhesion, without the commitment."
- A glue that doesn't actually bond, so you're never stuck with a decision.
- Taglines: "Glue-Free Glue — for when you're not sure." / "It's not sticking,
  and that's the point."
- Escalate: now with **Glue-Free Epoxy** ("removes itself at night").
- Slogan read as if it's a triumph: "Gluing has never been so un-committed!"

## 2. Pillowfort Mattress, by Thistledown Sleep Co.
"A bed so soft you lose track of Wednesday."
- Sells sleep so deep you forget appointments; the estate-planning division is
  "for when you finally wake up."
- Tagline: "Pillowfort — your tomorrow will still be there. Probably."
- Escalate: optional **Alarm Extension**, a subscription that makes your alarm
  sound like distant birdsong so you ignore it.

## 3. Fendwell Fencing
"Boundary, but make it neighborly."
- Fencing that politely ignores property lines when the wind blows.
- Tagline: "Fendwell — keeping your onions in and their goats out (usually)."
- Escalate: the **Fendwell Marshmallow Gate**, which opens for anyone carrying
  pie.

## 4. Novacalm (medication)
For "restless late-afternoon feelings." Side-effect list that grows over time:
- May cause drowsiness, ambition, an urge to re-roof the barn, or brief
  fluency in Latin.
- "Ask your doctor if Novacalm is right for you. Your doctor isn't sure either."
- The side-effect list is extended over episodes (ledger fact: "list last
  extended yesterday") — writers may add one new, absurd side effect per
  appearance.
