# The Town of Thistledown

A small fictional town where the station lives. Recurring locals can appear in
DJ talk-ups, commercials, and liners (RADIO.md §7).

**Geography:** Thistledown sits at the edge of Pilgrim Dot Farm; the radio
station operates out of the farmhouse. One volunteer bus ("the Thistledown
Spur"), a county fairground with a prize pig auction, a grain-silo record
studio, and a square with a clock tower that runs five minutes fast and the
whole town pretends is accurate.

**Recurring locals:**
- **Oona** — runs the general store; always knows who's getting a new roof.
- **The bus driver (named Dewy)** — the volunteer bus is chronically early or
  late, never on time.
- **Old Gant** — grows prize sweet corn; feuds with the county fair judges.
- **The fiddler at the auction** — appears only in hard times and at weddings.

**Tone rules:** warm, small-town, gently absurd. Never cruel to the locals;
the town is fond of its own eccentricities. No real towns, no real people, no
real businesses.
