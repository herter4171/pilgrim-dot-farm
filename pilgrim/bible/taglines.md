them as the station's voice, and feel free to say one verbatim when it fits a
liner or station ID.

## On-air notes

- **Profanity:** line 16 is a writers'-room line. The style guide bans profanity
  on air, so the runtime copy prompt uses "nonsense" in its place. If the
  operator lifts the ban, the original can air.
- **TTS:** write currency as words ("twenty thousand dollars") and keep the
  rest of the copy speakable. GPU and FCC are read as letters.
- **Facts:** the dollar and GPU-memory figures are creative exaggeration, not
  station accounting. Don't reuse them in the Tree Fiddy Report or any
  real-number copy.
- **Fourth wall:** these lines deliberately acknowledge that the station is
  AI-generated. That self-awareness is now part of the house voice; it is not a
  banned topic.
- **Second bank (2026-11-06):** line 38 reads "ninety degrees Celsius" on air
  (TTS keeps the canon's "90 C"). Line 43 assumes a big-game betting context; it
  fits the pregame/promo buckets best. Lines 48 and 54 reference live station
  features (turkey ratings, two-arrows requests) — if those features ship or
  change, recheck the copy. Line 55 is a dry historical reference, not cruelty
  copy; keep it out of the Tree Fiddy Report and news-adjacent slots.
