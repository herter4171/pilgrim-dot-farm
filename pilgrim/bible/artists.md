# Recurring Fictional Artists

Fictional artists with signature genres drawn from the song briefs. Their
song titles, "back catalog," and lore give the song pipeline (RADIO.md §6.2)
recurring characters whose gags can build across the day.

## 1. The Thistle Rakes
- **Genre:** bluegrass / old-time with a ceilidh stumble.
- **Lore:** a five-piece that "learned to play by watching the fiddler at the
  county fair's prize pig auction." No banjo, because the bass player's aunt
  says they're unlucky.
- **Signature:** songs about Saturdays, sweet corn, and the volunteer bus.
- Recurring title pattern: "The [Thing] of Thistledown."

## 2. Marrow & the Static
- **Genre:** synthwave / late-night drive-in.
- **Lore:** a duo who record in a grain silo with only a talk-radio antenna
  for a muse; every chorus has faint interference.
- **Signature:** neon-lit ballads about closing time and radio towers.
- Recurring title pattern: nouns that are also verb commands ("Reach,"
  "Transmit," "Leave the Light").

## 3. Rossie Pargetter
- **Genre:** bossa nova with farmyard horns.
- **Lore:** a singer-songwriter who "went to the city once," hated it, came
  back, and wrote about it for twenty years.
- **Signature:** breezy, wry songs about returning home and mild regret.
- Recurring title pattern: "Back to [Place] (Again)."

An artist may be invented fresh per brief, but prefer pulling from this bible
so the station develops a shared world.
